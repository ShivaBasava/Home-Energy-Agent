# Photovoltaics notes (illustrative)
Annual solar irradiation on a optimally tilted surface in Germany is roughly 1000-1200 kWh per square metre.
A well-oriented rooftop system in Germany typically yields 900-1000 kWh per installed kWp per year.
South-facing roofs at 30-40 degree tilt give the highest yield; east-west layouts lose about 10-15 percent but spread production over the day.
Performance ratio of modern systems is about 0.80-0.88, covering inverter, cable and temperature losses.
Partial shading from chimneys or trees can cut yield sharply unless module-level optimisers are used.
