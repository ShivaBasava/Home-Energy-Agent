# Battery storage notes (illustrative)
Without storage, a typical household self-consumes about 25-35 percent of its rooftop solar production.
A home battery of 1 kWh per 1 MWh of annual household demand raises self-consumption to around 55-70 percent.
Usable battery capacity is about 90 percent of nominal; round-trip efficiency is about 90 percent.
Oversizing the battery has diminishing returns because it rarely fully cycles in winter.
Lithium iron phosphate (LFP) cells are common in home storage because of cycle life above 6000 cycles.
