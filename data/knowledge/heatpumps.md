# Heat pump notes (illustrative)
Heat pump efficiency is expressed as seasonal performance factor (SPF); air-source units reach 3-4, ground-source 4-5.
Lower flow temperatures (underfloor heating, large radiators) improve efficiency significantly.
A heat pump adds roughly 3000-5000 kWh of annual electricity demand for a typical single-family house.
Combining a heat pump with rooftop solar helps in spring and autumn but little in midwinter.
Building insulation level and heat load calculation should be done before sizing the unit.
